# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want working on pamine to be a decent experience for everyone, regardless of
experience level, language, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation,
disability, appearance, or religion.

This is a hobby project about a game. Nobody is paid to be here, and nobody owes
anyone free labour. Keep that in mind in every interaction.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be direct, not rude.** Technical criticism is welcome and useful; contempt
  is not. Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements are about missing context, not
  malice. Ask before concluding someone was careless.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone was one. "Read the docs" is a fine
  answer only when paired with a link to the relevant part.
- **Stay on topic.** Issues and pull requests are for the project, not for
  unrelated arguments.
- **Respect the maintainer's time.** A clear reproduction and a minimal example
  get a fix far faster than a long description with no steps.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments about any group of people.
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly reopening a settled
  decision to wear people down.
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, commit messages, code
comments, and any chat or forum where you are representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to:

**rarekiller777@gmail.com**

Include what happened, where, and links or screenshots if you have them. Reports
are handled privately. The maintainer will read every report and respond as
quickly as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, the response will be one of:

1. **A private word** — a clarification of what was out of line.
2. **A public correction** — a request to edit or retract a comment.
3. **A temporary ban** from the project spaces.
4. **A permanent ban.**

The maintainer decides, and is the final word on enforcement. Retaliation
against someone who reported a problem is itself a violation and will be treated
as such.
